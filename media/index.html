SPREADING AWESOMENESS

Betsy Mendel has been featured across various media platforms sharing her inspiring journey and empowering message of transformation. From podcasts to interviews, she continues to spread the word about leaving mediocrity behind and embracing your awesomeness.

Featured Appearances Include:

  • Lifestyle and wellness podcasts
  • Author interviews and book features
  • Personal development speaking engagements
  • Health and fitness media coverage
  • Motivational speaking events
  • Book launch and promotional interviews

Betsy's authentic storytelling and practical approach to personal transformation resonates with audiences across all platforms. Her willingness to share her own struggles with depression, eating disorders, and exercise addiction creates genuine connections that inspire others to take action in their own lives.

Speaking Topics Include:

  • Overcoming personal struggles and mental health challenges
  • Transforming limiting beliefs into empowering actions
  • Building healthy lifestyle habits that last
  • Finding purpose and passion in everyday life
  • The journey from mediocrity to awesomeness
